    What the agent will not do

    • Say anything before the person confirms they are the member.
    • Talk about anyone else's record: it is only ever given one.
    • Take a payment, a PIN, a password or an account number.
    • Speak a sentence that is not in the approved Amharic script.
    • Call a member who has no recorded consent for calls.
